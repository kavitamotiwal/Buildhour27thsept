# HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund - Direct Plan Growth

HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund Direct Plan Growth is an Equity Mutual Fund (ELSS) scheme launched by HDFC Mutual Fund on 10 Dec 1999. It is rated Very High Risk.

## Lock-in Period

HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund is an ELSS scheme with a lock-in period of 3 years from the date of allotment. The Groww page carries an "ELSS - 3Y Lock-in" badge.

## Expense Ratio

The expense ratio of HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund - Direct Plan Growth is 1.21%.

## Exit Load

Exit load is Nil.

## Minimum Investment

Minimum SIP investment is Rs. 500. Minimum lumpsum / 1st investment is Rs. 500. Minimum for 2nd investment is Rs. 500.

## Riskometer

The riskometer level of HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund - Direct Plan Growth is Very High Risk.

## Benchmark

The benchmark index of HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund - Direct Plan Growth is the NIFTY 500 Total Return Index.

## Investment Objective

The scheme seeks to generate capital appreciation / income from a portfolio, comprising predominantly of equity & equity related instruments.
