# HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund - Direct Growth

HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund Direct Growth is a Hybrid Mutual Fund scheme launched by HDFC Mutual Fund on 10 Dec 1999. It is rated Very High Risk.

## Expense Ratio

The expense ratio of HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund - Direct Growth is 0.78%.

## Exit Load

Exit load for units in excess of 15% of the investment, 1% will be charged for redemption within 1 year.

## Minimum Investment

Minimum SIP investment is Rs. 100. Minimum lumpsum / 1st investment is Rs. 100. Minimum for 2nd investment is Rs. 100.

## Riskometer

The riskometer level of HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund - Direct Growth is Very High Risk.

## Benchmark

The benchmark index of HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund - Direct Growth is the NIFTY 50 Hybrid Composite Debt 50:50 Index.

## Investment Objective

The scheme seeks to provide long-term capital appreciation / income from a dynamic mix of equity and debt investments.

## Fund Facts

NAV as of 28 Sep '26 is Rs. 551.04. Fund size (AUM) is Rs. 1,07,295.79 Cr. The scheme's rating is 5 out of 5. The scheme was launched on 10 Dec 1999.
