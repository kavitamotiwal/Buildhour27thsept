# HDFC Small Cap Fund - Direct Growth

HDFC Small Cap Fund Direct Growth is an Equity Mutual Fund scheme launched by HDFC Mutual Fund on 10 Dec 1999. It is rated Very High Risk.

## Expense Ratio

The expense ratio of HDFC Small Cap Fund - Direct Growth is 0.78%.

## Exit Load

Exit load of 1% if redeemed within 1 year.

## Minimum Investment

Minimum SIP investment is Rs. 100. Minimum lumpsum / 1st investment is Rs. 100. Minimum for 2nd investment is Rs. 100.

## Riskometer

The riskometer level of HDFC Small Cap Fund - Direct Growth is Very High Risk.

## Benchmark

The benchmark index of HDFC Small Cap Fund - Direct Growth is the BSE 250 SmallCap Total Return Index.

## Investment Objective

The scheme seeks to provide long-term capital appreciation / income by investing predominantly in Small-Cap companies.

## Fund Facts

NAV as of 28 Sep '26 is Rs. 157.26. Fund size (AUM) is Rs. 41,890.86 Cr. The scheme's rating is 3 out of 5. The scheme was launched on 10 Dec 1999.
